# Prompts de vídeo no estilo 3D fofo (Gemini / Veo)

Como usar:
1. No Gemini, escolha **gerar vídeo** e, se der, anexe como imagem de referência a **logo** (`kit2/logo-final.png`) e/ou a **bonequinha** (ex.: `kit3/tablet.png`).
2. Cole um prompt abaixo. Eles estão em inglês porque o gerador entende melhor. O texto que aparece no vídeo está em português entre aspas.
3. Se o texto sair torto, gere sem texto (apague a parte "On-screen text") e escreva por cima no Instagram ou no CapCut.
4. Formato: vertical 9:16, 8 a 10 segundos.

Estilo base, que já está em todos os prompts: *soft 3D render, pastel mint green, pink and chocolate brown, cozy kawaii aesthetic, glossy toy-like materials, soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, slow cinematic camera.*

---

## 1 · Vitrine de doces (confeitaria)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, cozy kawaii pastel aesthetic in mint green, pink and chocolate brown. A glass bakery display case with a pastel sign on top showing the logo "Ternura" (use the reference logo). Inside, tiny glossy desserts: chocolate heart cake with pink bow, mint ice cream cone, macarons, a smartphone and a laptop made of candy showing cute pastel websites. Slow dolly-in camera toward the case, a cake-server hand gently picks up a heart-shaped cookie that reads "seu site" and places it in a pink box with a bow. Sparkles. Soft studio lighting, shallow depth of field, glossy plastic and fondant textures.
On-screen text at top: "Seu site saindo do forno ♡"
```

## 2 · Caixinha de presente (unboxing)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, kawaii pastel mint, pink and chocolate brown. Top-down then slow push-in on a pastel pink gift box with a chocolate-brown satin ribbon and a tag with the "Ternura" logo. The ribbon unties by itself, the lid lifts, confetti and tiny hearts pop out, revealing a miniature mint smartphone inside showing a cute pastel website, surrounded by tiny macarons and a heart keychain. Gentle sparkle, cozy soft lighting, glossy toy materials.
On-screen text: "Abre o seu presente: um site novo ♡"
```

## 3 · Máquina de chiclete (gumball)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, cozy kawaii pastel mint, pink and chocolate brown. A cute retro gumball machine with the "Ternura" logo on the base. A small hand turns the handle, a glossy pastel capsule rolls down the chute and pops open, revealing tiny objects one after another: a mini logo sticker, a mini phone with a pastel Instagram feed, a mini laptop with a website. Camera slowly follows the capsule. Soft studio light, shallow depth of field, satisfying ASMR feel.
On-screen text: "Qual vai ser a sua marca?"
```

## 4 · Quarto da bonequinha (setup)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render of a cozy kawaii designer bedroom in pastel mint, pink and chocolate brown. A cute stylized girl with short brown hair with mint highlights, glasses, pink hair clips, mint hoodie with brown fur collar (use reference image) sits at a desk with a pastel computer, designing a logo on screen. Fairy lights, plush toys, heart pillows, mint-chocolate ice cream on the desk. Slow orbit camera around the desk, the screen shows a website appearing step by step. Warm soft lighting.
On-screen text: "Enquanto você dorme, eu crio sua marca ♡"
```

## 5 · Fliperama / joguinho
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, kawaii pastel mint, pink and chocolate brown. A pastel arcade cabinet with the "Ternura" logo on the marquee. On the screen, a cute pixel game where a small character collects hearts labeled "site", "logo", "posts". A hand presses the glossy pink buttons, the screen flashes "LEVEL UP", and a tiny trophy shaped like a heart pops out of the coin slot. Slow push-in camera, soft studio lighting, glossy plastic toy materials.

```

## 6 · Carta / envelope
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, kawaii pastel mint, pink and chocolate brown. A pastel envelope sealed with a pink heart wax seal stamped with the "Ternura" logo lies on a mint lace tablecloth. The seal pops, the envelope opens and a letter slides out and unfolds, then turns into a tiny 3D website and a phone with a pastel feed. Floating hearts and sparkles, slow camera push-in, soft cozy lighting.

```

## 7 · Máquina de pelúcia (variação do seu vídeo)
```
Vertical 9:16 video, soft 3D render, pastel claw machine in mint and pink with the "Ternura" logo on top, inside filled with tiny glossy items: chocolate heart bag with pink bow, mint ice cream, mini phone and mini laptop showing pastel websites, heart keychains. The claw grabs a heart keychain that reads "seu site novo", drops it in the prize door, the door opens with a soft glow and sparkles. Slow dolly-in, cozy soft lighting, shallow depth of field.
On-screen text: "Pegou? É seu ♡"
```

---

Dicas:
- Não coloque marcas de outras empresas (ex.: iPod, Sylvanian Families) nos vídeos de anúncio, porque o Instagram pode recusar.
- Gere 2 ou 3 vezes o mesmo prompt e escolha a melhor versão.
- Música em alta + legenda curta com pergunta ("qual você escolheria?") ajudam o alcance.
